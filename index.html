About the Event

Test Your Limits

KHATRON KE KHILADI is a competitive, team-based indoor event designed to test participants’ problem-solving ability, concentration, coordination, observation, logical thinking and teamwork.

Each team consists of seven members and takes part in three different challenges. The event combines an individual puzzle task with two group-based challenges, ensuring that participants contribute both independently and as a team.

Conducted in a controlled college environment using simple materials, the challenges are engaging and competitive while maintaining participant safety.

🎯

Objectives

  • Test individual problem-solving & concentration
  • Build teamwork & coordination
  • Encourage observation & logical thinking
  • Provide a safe competitive experience
👥

Who Can Join

College students who can form teams of seven members. Ideal for those who love puzzles, team challenges and competitive activities.

The Rounds

Three Challenges

Every round demands focus, communication and smart thinking.

01

Puzzle Challenge

Individual • Problem-solving • Concentration

One member from each team solves a puzzle under a strict 2-minute time limit. The remaining six members may not assist. Tests focus and the ability to work under pressure.

  • Only one selected member solves
  • 2 minutes maximum
  • No hints from teammates
02

Cup Pyramid Challenge

Team • Coordination • Communication

All seven members must actively participate to build a cup pyramid using only the materials provided. If it collapses, the team rebuilds. Pure teamwork under time pressure.

  • All 7 members must take part
  • Only organiser-supplied materials
  • Rebuild if the pyramid falls
03

Crack the Clue

Team • Observation • Logic

Teams receive a set of images. Each image holds a hint. Members discuss, connect the clues and submit the final answer within the time limit. No phones, no external help.

  • Observe all image clues carefully
  • Full team discussion required
  • Submit final answer on time
Fair Play

Rules & Regulations

General Rules

  • Each team must have exactly 7 members.
  • Follow all instructions given by organisers and game coordinators.
  • No external assistance, mobile phones or unfair aids during challenges.
  • Organisers’ decisions are final on all matters of scoring and eligibility.
  • Respect safety guidelines — no pushing, running or unsafe behaviour.

Round-Specific

  • Puzzle: Only the chosen member may touch/solve. Time limit 2 minutes.
  • Cup Pyramid: All members must participate. Rebuild if it collapses. Use only provided cups.
  • Crack the Clue: All members discuss. Submit answer within the announced time. No phones.

Safety & Fair Play

  • Games are skill challenges, not dangerous stunts.
  • Lightweight cups and stable tables only.
  • Activity stops immediately if any safety concern arises.
  • Same instructions and judging for every team.
